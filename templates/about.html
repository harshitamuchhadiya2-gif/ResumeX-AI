System Architecture & Engineering

Inside ResumeX Pro

ResumeX Pro is an intelligent resume parsing, scoring, and skill-gap detection engine engineered with Python, Flask, and Scikit-Learn. Designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

01

PDF Text Extraction

Utilizes PyPDF2 to parse multi-page binary PDF resumes into structured, machine-readable plain text with UTF-8 normalization.

02

NLP Text Preprocessing

Applies lowercasing, regex-based punctuation cleaning, and selective stop-word filtering to preserve technical compound phrases.

03

TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity

Calculates multi-dimensional term vectors and measures the cosine angle between resume and job description representations.

04

Weighted Scoring Model

Blends 50% skill overlap, 25% semantic cosine similarity, 15% resume structure quality, and 10% keyword density.
